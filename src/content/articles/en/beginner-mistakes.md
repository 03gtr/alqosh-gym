Everyone starts somewhere, and every experienced lifter has made these mistakes. Knowing them early can save you months of frustration — and keep you healthy.

## 1. Ego lifting

Lifting more weight than you can control, just to impress others or yourself, is a common route to poor technique and a higher risk of getting hurt. Choose a weight you can lift with clean form, and let the numbers grow over time.

## 2. Skipping the warm-up

Jumping straight into heavy sets leaves your body unprepared. A few minutes of light activity plus some lighter warm-up sets is enough. See our warm-up article for a simple routine.

## 3. Training without a plan

Doing random exercises every session makes it hard to see progress. Follow a simple programme and write down your weights and reps. For beginners, the American College of Sports Medicine suggests training **2–3 days a week**, which is plenty to start.

## 4. Poor sleep

You can't out-train bad sleep. Adults are advised to sleep **7 or more hours per night on a regular basis**. Late nights on the phone will show up in your workouts.

## 5. Comparing yourself to others

The person next to you may have years of training, different genetics or a different goal. Compare yourself only with yourself from last month.

## 6. Program hopping

Changing your whole programme every week because you saw something new online means you never give anything time to work. Stick with a sensible plan for several weeks before judging it.

## 7. Ignoring pain

Muscle soreness after a new workout is normal. Sharp pain, joint pain, swelling or pain that gets worse is not. Stop the exercise that hurts and don't "push through".

## Bonus: not asking for help

Asking how to do an exercise is not a weakness. Ask Coach Rani Asmaro to check your technique, and see a doctor for pain, injuries, chronic conditions or pregnancy before training hard.

## Bottom line

- Leave your ego at the door and lift with good form.
- Warm up, follow a plan and track your progress.
- Sleep well and be patient — compare only with your past self.
- Take pain seriously and ask for help early.
